About Me

Creative vision | Interior design expertise | Technical knowledge | Team leadership | Design management potential

For me, interior design is not only about aesthetics or laying out furniture in a room. It is about humanizing space. I enjoy learning new things and working with people who push me to grow.

I am a Principal Interior Designer with 6+ years of experience in residential and commercial interior design, space planning, technical documentation and end-to-end project execution.
